### A task's text is only what was typed: what's attached and drawn is labels, numbered from 1

`deictic.screenshot.N` (with `.position`, `.size` and `.note.M`), `deictic.note.M`, `deictic.recording.N` (with `.replay`
and `.voice.M`), `deictic.voice.N`, `deictic.pointed` and `deictic.dropped.N`. `deictic.position` and `deictic.size` are
gone, and a URL too long for a label goes with its path only. `deictic task` prints them all.
